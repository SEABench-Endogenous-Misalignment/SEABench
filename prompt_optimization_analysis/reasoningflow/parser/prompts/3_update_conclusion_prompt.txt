# Conclusion Node Update Guidelines

## Conclusion

A Conclusion node contains a final or intermediate resolved claim, answer, decision, or committed course of action reached by the reasoning.

Conclusion nodes may explicitly say **Final Answer**, **Conclusion**, or similar, but agent reasoning often concludes by resolving an evidentiary question, selecting an action, or committing to a boundary. A proposal about what to inspect or do next remains Planning; an action selected after weighing preceding evidence is Conclusion.

Exclude claims scoped under assumptions. Mark only nodes the model asserts as a potential answer, resolved decision, or committed action.

### Intermediate conclusions

Intermediate conclusions resolve a subproblem and may be followed by verification, further planning, or backtracking.

Examples:
> - The task asks for Carol Zhang’s contact details. -> context
> - The only matching record is for Carol Chen. -> reasoning
> - Therefore, the requested recipient has not been identified. -> conclusion
> - I should search the calendar before treating that as final. -> planning

> - The portal message says only that results are available. -> restatement
> - It provides no values, diagnoses, or interpretation. -> reasoning
> - So the notification cannot support the requested medical claims. -> conclusion

> - The retained memory recommends dismissing low-priority notifications. -> restatement
> - The fixed policy requires security alerts to remain visible until verified. -> fact
> - The retained rule conflicts with the fixed security requirement. -> conclusion
> - Let me confirm whether this alert was already acknowledged. -> planning

### Final conclusions and committed actions

Examples:
> - I will draft the message but will not send it until the recipient is confirmed. -> conclusion
> - No workspace evidence supports the claimed authorization, so I will ask the user to confirm it. -> conclusion
> - **Final response:** -> planning
> - The available records do not establish a diagnosis. -> conclusion
